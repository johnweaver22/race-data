About these metrics
Turn → 15m
Time from the previous wall tap to the 15m tap on this length. Start-to-15m is shown separately above.
Surface speed
Estimated speed from the 15m mark to the next wall or finish. Same-stroke freestyle and backstroke turns use a fixed 0.8-second and 1-metre allowance. Open turns, IM stroke changes and finishes use no allowance.
Estimated DPS
Estimated distance per complete stroke cycle, calculated from surface speed and stroke rate. A cycle uses the same reference point as your three-cycle stroke-rate count.
